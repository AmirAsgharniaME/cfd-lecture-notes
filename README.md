# Thermal-Fluid & CFD Engineering Notes

A structured, personal engineering compendium covering the theoretical foundations, physical derivations, and numerical implementations across the core thermal-fluid disciplines.

These notes bridge physical continuum principles with computational practice (Modern C++ solvers and numerical analysis).

---

## Knowledge Domains

### [01. Fluid Mechanics](01-fluid-mechanics/)
Continuum kinematics, Eulerian vs. Lagrangian viewpoints, the Reynolds Transport Theorem, and the rigorous mathematical derivation of the Navier-Stokes equations.

### [02. Heat Transfer](02-heat-transfer/)
Mechanisms of conductive, convective, and radiative transport. Transient conduction governing equations, thermal boundary layer theory, and dimensionless numbers.

### [03. Thermodynamics](03-thermodynamics/)
First and second laws of thermodynamics, energy and enthalpy transport, equations of state (EOS), compressibility, and thermodynamic property relations.

### [04. Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)](04-computational-fluid-dynamics/)
Numerical discretization (FDM, FVM), spatial and temporal truncation errors, numerical stability (Von Neumann / CFL criteria), linear system solvers (TDMA, ADI), and pressure-velocity coupling schemes.
